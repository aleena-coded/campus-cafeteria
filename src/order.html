How to Order

Choose your meal, pay at the counter, and pick it up with your order token.

Steps to Order

  1. Choose your items

    Check the menu or today's deals and decide what you want before you reach the counter.

  2. Pay at the counter

    Tell the cashier your order and pay at the counter. The order is confirmed only after payment.

  3. Collect your order token

    Take the token with your order number and keep it until your meal is handed to you.

Guidelines

Peak hours

The counter is busiest on weekdays from 12:30 PM to 2:30 PM. Come a little earlier or later for a shorter queue. Opening hours stay the same as on the home page: Monday to Friday, 8:00 AM to 6:00 PM.

Pick-up rules

  • Wait by the serving window until your token number is called.
  • Show your token when you collect the order.
  • Pick up within 15 minutes of the call. Uncollected meals may be set aside.
  • One token covers one paid order. Do not hand it to another student.

At the counter

Have your choices ready. Staff will not hold an unpaid order. If an item has run out, they will tell you before you pay so you can choose something else.

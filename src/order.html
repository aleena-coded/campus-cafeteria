How to Order

Steps to Order

  1. Choose your items from the menu.
  2. Pay at the counter to confirm your order.
  3. Collect your order token and wait for your number to be called.

Guidelines

  • Peak hours are typically 12:00 PM to 2:00 PM. Expect slightly longer wait times.
  • Keep your token safe and present it at pick-up.
  • Please collect your order promptly once your token number is announced.
